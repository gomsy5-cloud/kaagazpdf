Contact KaagazPDF

We usually reply within 1–2 working days

Have a question, found a bug, or want to suggest a tool? We would genuinely love to hear from you. KaagazPDF is built and run in India, and real people read every message.

Email us

The fastest way to reach us is by email. Write to us at support@kaagazpdf.in and we will get back to you, usually within one to two working days.

Tip: To help us solve your issue quickly, please tell us which tool you were using (for example, "Compress PDF" or "Aadhaar Masking"), what device and browser you are on (Android Chrome, iPhone Safari, Windows, etc.), and what happened. A short description goes a long way.

What you can contact us about

  • Help using a tool — if something is not working the way you expect, or you are not sure which tool to use for your task.
  • Bug reports — if a tool gives an error or an unexpected result. Since every file is processed privately on your own device, please never email us your actual documents; just describe the problem.
  • Feature requests — is there a document or photo task you wish KaagazPDF could do? Tell us. Many of our tools exist because users asked for them.
  • Feedback & suggestions — about the design, the guides, translations, or anything else. We read it all.
  • Business & partnerships — for collaborations or press, email us with a short note about what you have in mind.

A quick note on privacy

KaagazPDF processes your files entirely inside your own browser — your documents are never uploaded to our servers, and they are never attached to your account. Because of this, we cannot see your files and cannot recover them for you. If you are writing about a problem with a specific file, please describe what happened rather than sending the file itself. Your privacy matters to us, and this design is the reason people trust KaagazPDF with sensitive documents like Aadhaar and PAN copies.

Response time

We are a small team, so please allow one to two working days for a reply. During busy periods it may take a little longer, but we do our best to answer every genuine message. If you do not hear back, it is worth checking your spam folder — replies from support@kaagazpdf.in occasionally land there.

Frequently asked before contacting

Is KaagazPDF really free?

Yes — during our beta, all 27 tools are free and unlimited. You only need a quick free login to use them. You can read more on our About page.

Are my documents safe?

Absolutely. Files are processed on your device using in-browser technology and are never uploaded. You can read our full privacy policy for details.

How do I do a specific task?

Check our free guides first — we have step-by-step help for common tasks like compressing a PDF, resizing an exam photo, masking an Aadhaar number and more.
