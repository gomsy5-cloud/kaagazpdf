Why these guides exist

Millions of Indians fill online forms every month — for exams, passports, PAN cards, scholarships, jobs and admissions — and the single most frustrating step is almost always the document upload. The size limits are tiny, the pixel requirements are precise, and one wrong setting means starting over. We built KaagazPDF to make that step effortless, and we wrote these guides to explain the "why" behind each requirement so you get it right the first time, every time.

We also care deeply about privacy. Documents like Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements and mark sheets contain sensitive personal information, and uploading them to a random website is a real risk. That is why every tool referenced in these guides works entirely inside your own browser — your file is opened, edited and saved on your device, and is never sent to any server. You can even switch off your internet after the page loads and the tools keep working.
